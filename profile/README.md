# Mosticare

> The planet needs a green solution for mosquito-free life. Time to stop spraying chemicals.
> Adrian Christiansen, Founder

Mosticare works to end mosquito-borne disease through honest information, real-time surveillance, and vector control that never poisons the nature it defends.

It is two organisations under one name, kept separate by design:

- **Mosticare Foundation** (Mosticare Ühing, registered Estonian non-profit, reg. 80674126) runs [mosticare.org](https://mosticare.org): plain-language knowledge on mosquito-borne disease, a live European threat map built from public agency data, and a growing public-health library. The Foundation makes no commercial claims and sells nothing.
- **Mosticare OÜ** (reg. 17360990), the for-profit arm, runs the storefront at [mosticare.com](https://www.mosticare.com): physical mosquito barriers, nets and screens. Commerce funds the mission, never the other way around.

## What we're against

Chemical repellents and sprays sold as the default answer: coils, candles, plug-in vaporisers, and aerosols that expose people to substances they never needed, and uncertified marketplace nets that fail at the mesh. We call this out at category level, with sources, on [mosticare.org](https://mosticare.org).

## What we're building

Physical barriers that stop mosquito bites without adding toxins to homes or ecosystems, and the honest public record that helps people choose protection that works.

## This organisation

This GitHub organisation hosts the code and content infrastructure behind mosticare.org and mosticare.com. Most repositories are private; individual tools may be open-sourced as the platform matures.
